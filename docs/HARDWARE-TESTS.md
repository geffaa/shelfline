# Hardware and integration test list

Everything here was built and tested **without** the real device unless a row says otherwise. A blank "Result" means it has not been tried on real hardware. When it is, write down the device model, the date, what happened, and update the claim in `docs/TECH-SPEC.md` section 10 and the README.

## Not built, on purpose
- Printing while the till is offline: the printer is reached through the store's bridge by the server, so it needs a connection. A native app could talk to the printer directly; that is future work.
- Card payments while offline: a terminal refuses without a network; the till falls back to recording a card payment by hand, flagged as before.
- Real card reader certification, receipts with card scheme data, tips, refunds to the card (a refund is recorded, the money is returned by hand).
